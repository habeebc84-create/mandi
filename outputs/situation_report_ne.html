प्राविधिक सीमाहरू तथा सावधानीहरू (Technical Limitations):
  • Revisit time of Sentinel-1/2 (several days) precludes sub-hourly early warning or flash-flood prediction.
  • Radar layover, shadow, and steep Himalayan slopes can cause blind spots or false positives.
  • Wet snow, saturated soils, and topographic shadows mimic water backscatter in C-band SAR.
  • Debris and sediment deposit detection is heuristic change-detection without dedicated labeled training sets.
  • 10-meter pixel resolution is coarse for single rural huts, footbridges, or narrow mountain paths.
  • OpenStreetMap coverage in the Himalaya is incomplete; absence of a road does not guarantee absence on the ground.
  • Satellite masks do not provide water depth, flow velocity, or road surface structural integrity.
  • This system is an emergency educational prototype, not an officially certified operational life-safety tool.
